Accounts Payable (AP) fraud is no longer just a compliance risk — it is an AI arms race. Fraudsters are using the same technology that powers modern automation to create convincing invoices, impersonate suppliers, and exploit manual processes at scale. The question is whether your AP defenses are keeping pace.

## The New Face of Accounts Payable Fraud

Traditional AP fraud schemes — duplicate invoices, fake vendors, payment diversion — are now being amplified by artificial intelligence. Fraudsters are using AI-powered tools to generate realistic invoices within seconds, mimic supplier communication styles, and manipulate payment requests at a scale that manual controls simply cannot match.

Yet only 17% of organizations currently use AI to combat fraud — meaning the vast majority are fighting an AI-powered threat with manual controls designed for a different era.

## How AI Is Empowering Fraudsters

Historically, AP fraud required significant effort. Fraudsters had to manually create fake invoices, forge documents, or gather insider information. Today, AI tools can generate highly realistic invoices in seconds, mimic supplier communication styles and email formats, create convincing payment change requests, analyze publicly available company information to target specific organizations, and scale fraudulent activities across hundreds of companies simultaneously.

As AI technology becomes more accessible, the sophistication and volume of fraud attempts continues to increase — while the cost to execute them falls.

## Common AP Fraud Risks Growing in the AI Era

Vendor Impersonation

AI-generated emails closely resemble legitimate supplier communications. A simple request to update banking details can redirect payments to fraudulent accounts.

Invoice Manipulation

AI can generate invoices that appear identical to authentic supplier documents, making manual review increasingly ineffective at catching them.

Duplicate Invoice Fraud

Fraudsters submit slightly modified versions of legitimate invoices, knowing manual processes will miss subtle variations in invoice numbers or amounts.

Business Email Compromise

AI-generated messages imitate executives, procurement managers, or suppliers with remarkable accuracy — pressuring AP teams to process urgent payments.

Synthetic Vendors

Fraudulent vendor records created using AI-generated business identities can pass basic onboarding controls, establishing a foothold inside your supplier master.

## Why Traditional AP Controls Are No Longer Enough

Most organizations still rely on manual invoice reviews, spreadsheet-based validations, random audits, basic ERP controls, and reactive fraud investigations. While these controls remain important, they were designed for a different threat landscape.

When AP teams process thousands of invoices monthly, manual review cannot keep pace with AI-generated fraud attempts. Human reviewers will overlook subtle inconsistencies that advanced technology can identify instantly — and fraudsters know it.

**The gap is widening:** 76% of organizations were targeted by payment fraud in 2025, yet only 17% use AI to fight it. That gap is exactly where fraudsters operate.
